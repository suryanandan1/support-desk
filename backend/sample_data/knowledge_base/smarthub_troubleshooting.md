# SmartHub Troubleshooting Guide

Acme Home Devices is a fictional company used for demonstration purposes.

## The SmartHub will not connect to Wi-Fi
1. Make sure your router broadcasts a 2.4 GHz network. The SmartHub does not support 5 GHz-only networks.
2. Keep the SmartHub within 10 metres of the router during setup.
3. Restart the SmartHub by unplugging it for 10 seconds.
4. If it still fails, hold the reset button for 15 seconds until the light flashes orange, then run setup again in the Acme app.

## What the status light means
- Solid green: connected and working normally.
- Flashing blue: ready for setup.
- Flashing orange: resetting.
- Solid red: the SmartHub has no internet connection.

## Devices show as offline
Check that the device has power and is within 30 metres of the SmartHub. Replace the batteries in battery-powered sensors. If several devices go offline at once, restart the SmartHub.

## Updating the firmware
Firmware updates install automatically overnight. To update manually, open the Acme app, go to Devices > SmartHub > Firmware, and tap Check for updates.

## Factory reset
A factory reset erases all paired devices and settings. Hold the reset button for 30 seconds until the light turns solid orange.
