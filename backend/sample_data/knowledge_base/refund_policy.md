# Returns and Refunds Policy

Acme Home Devices is a fictional company used for demonstration purposes.
Last updated: 1 September 2026.

## Return window
You can return most products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Gift cards
- Products marked as "final sale"
- Opened software licences and digital downloads
- Batteries that have been installed in a device

## Damaged or defective items
If your item arrives damaged, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos of the damage. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including the original shipping cost.

## How to start a return
1. Sign in to your account and open Order history.
2. Select the order and choose Return items.
3. Print the prepaid return label and drop the parcel at any partner courier location.

Return shipping is free for orders delivered within the United States. International customers pay for return shipping unless the item was damaged or defective.

## Refund processing time
Once we receive and inspect your return, we approve the refund within 2 business days. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

## Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement.
