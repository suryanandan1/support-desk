Acme Protect subscription plans

Acme Home Devices is a fictional company used for demonstration purposes.

Acme Protect adds cloud video storage and professional monitoring to your devices. All plans are billed monthly or yearly and can be cancelled at any time.

Basic plan: $4.99 per month or $49.99 per year. Includes 7 days of cloud video history for up to 2 cameras and mobile alerts.

Plus plan: $9.99 per month or $99.99 per year. Includes 30 days of cloud video history for unlimited cameras, mobile alerts, smart person detection, and an extended warranty on all registered devices.

Pro plan: $19.99 per month or $199.99 per year. Includes 60 days of cloud video history for unlimited cameras, 24/7 professional monitoring with emergency dispatch, and an extended warranty on all registered devices.

Free trial: new customers get a 30-day free trial of the Plus plan. You will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

Cancelling a subscription: cancel at any time under Settings > Subscriptions. Monthly plans stay active until the end of the current billing month. Yearly plans cancelled within the first 30 days receive a prorated refund. After 30 days, yearly plans are not refunded but stay active until the end of the billing year.

Payment methods: we accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept cryptocurrency such as Bitcoin, and we do not accept cheques.
