# Shipping and Delivery

Acme Home Devices is a fictional company used for demonstration purposes.

## Order processing time
Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, at weekends, or on public holidays ship the next business day.

## Shipping options in the United States
- Standard shipping: delivery in 3 to 5 business days. It costs $5.99 and is free on orders over $50.
- Express shipping: delivery in 1 to 2 business days for $14.99.
- Next-day shipping: delivery the next business day for orders placed before noon Eastern Time, for $24.99.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and costs a flat $19.99. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

We do not currently ship to any other countries.

## Tracking your order
You will receive an email with a tracking number when your order ships. You can also find the tracking details under Order history in your account.

## Lost or delayed packages
If your tracking has not updated for 7 days, or your package is marked as delivered but you cannot find it, contact support. We will open an investigation with the courier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement at no cost.

## Changing your shipping address
You can change the shipping address until the order ships. Open the order in Order history and choose Edit address. After the order ships, the address can no longer be changed.
